# Code of Conduct

This project is for anyone who cares about designing good experiences, whatever their background, role, or level of experience. We want everyone to feel welcome here.

## Our standard

In issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other project space:

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intentions.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and make it specific and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Accept that others may disagree, and keep disagreements focused on the resource, not on individuals.
- Credit other people's work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information, and spam or repeated self-promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions, comments, and issues that don't follow this code of conduct, and may block people who repeatedly break it. If you experience or witness behavior that violates it, please report it to the maintainers through a private channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled respectfully and with discretion.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is short and in plain language on purpose. For a more detailed community standard, see the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
